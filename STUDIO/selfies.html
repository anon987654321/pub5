This gallery reads selfies/manifest.js, which photograph/selfies_gallery.rb writes from the renders in lora/. Render a series first, then rebuild the manifest:

ruby STUDIO/photograph.rb --selfie-series --subject ragnhild
ruby STUDIO/photograph/selfies_gallery.rb

Animate a still into a film with the first-frame image-to-video engine, and lay the soundtrack under it in the same pass:

ruby STUDIO/photograph.rb --animate lora/<still>.jpg --subject ragnhild \
  --prompt "she turns to the lens and smiles" --soundtrack selfies/dilla.mp3

STUDIO — private subject media. Renders are not tracked; the manifest is generated.
